# Surface brief: pavcraft.com home (/)

Mode: Persuade (brand catalogue). Audience: Ukrainian small-business owners who run things themselves (ФОП, sellers, service masters), mostly on phones, arriving from chats. Job: route each visitor to the tool for their field in seconds; second, invite them to say what routine to remove. Field-neutral: the home must host tools whose own pages have their own worlds (Pricing Kit = receipt). Proof on hand: one tool in beta; no customers or reviews.

Owner feedback 2026-11-09 (binding): the wall is a stairwell (під'їзд) wall, not a poster hoarding; fonts handwritten; marker scribbles on the wall; tear line must not move when a tab peels and the wall must show under a lifted or torn tab; torn tabs have no backing or shadow; every paper can be peeled off and re-stuck elsewhere.

## Direction contract

THESIS: Pavcraft's catalogue is the notice board nobody authorised: paper notices taped to a Ukrainian stairwell wall, each tool one notice with tear-off tabs, and tearing a tab takes you to it. The page refuses the SaaS grid of identical tool cards and the dark tech hero.

OWN-WORLD: Stairwell wall: whitewash (#E7E3D9) on the upper band, a dark trim stripe (#3F5F55), oil-painted green panel (#6E9686) below, fine plaster grain over both. Marker scribbles on the wall in black, red (#C62A22) and blue (#1F47B8), including the classic "ОГОЛОШЕННЯ НЕ КЛЕЇТИ!". Notices on white office paper (#FBFAF6) and yellowed paper (#EAE4D3), translucent crimped masking tape, ballpoint blue (#1F47B8) as the only action colour. All type handwritten: Pangolin (felt-tip print) for headings, tabs and controls; Caveat (ballpoint and marker) for text and scribbles. Notices rotate up to 4 degrees; shadows follow the paper's own shape (no backing panels).

STORY: The visitor sees a stairwell wall of notices, recognises the form instantly, reads that each notice is a tool that removes one manual chore, finds the one for their field, tears a tab or taps the notice to open it; if nothing fits, the "Що вам заважає?" notice's tabs open an email. They can peel any paper by its tape and stick it elsewhere.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Taped "PAVCRAFT / інструменти для малого бізнесу" slip top left and a taped "Написати" slip top right; the handwritten headline "Оголошення для тих, хто все робить сам" directly on the whitewash with "ОГОЛОШЕННЯ НЕ КЛЕЇТИ!" scrawled in red beside it; a taped note under it; then, on the green panel, the Pricing Kit notice (heading "Pricing Kit / для продавців Prom", "Відкрити Pricing Kit →" link, tear-off tabs "Pricing Kit", one already torn to a ragged stub) and the "Що вам заважає?" notice beside it on desktop; on phones the Pricing Kit notice with its link is in view.

FORM: Stairwell notice board with tear-off notices, fused from the dealt challenger design-canon-decollage-torn-billboard (paper layered on a wall, the tear as the control) and revised to the owner's stairwell brief. seed:29c358412a09147b. Raises kept: future tools present as yellowed older notices with "за попитом" tabs ("скоро" dropped: it implied a promise against the demand-check rule); one fixed address per tool (its tab label).

SIGNATURE: Peel and re-stick: every paper lifts by its tape (mouse can also drag the paper itself), follows the pointer with a larger shadow, and sticks where dropped at a new slight angle; positions persist for the viewer, and "Повісити все як було" resets them; arrow keys move a focused tape. Tabs peel on hover around a fixed cut line and tear off on click before navigating. Motion is skipped under reduced motion; links work with no script.
