# Surface brief: pavcraft.com home (/)

Mode: Persuade (brand catalogue). Audience: Ukrainian small-business owners who run things themselves (ФОП, sellers, service masters), mostly on phones, arriving from chats. Job: route each visitor to the tool for their field in seconds; second, invite them to say what routine to remove. Field-neutral: the home must host tools whose own pages have their own worlds (Pricing Kit = receipt). Proof on hand: one tool in beta; no customers or reviews.

Owner pinned this direction on 2026-11-09 ("make your own variant, keep only notices and tear-off tabs"). Binding from the owner: flat acid-lime board instead of the stairwell wall; handwritten fonts; rude Ukrainian marker scribbles censored by peelable stickers (explicit request); everything draggable; the tear line must not move when a tab peels and the board shows under a lifted or torn tab; torn tabs have no backing or shadow.

## Direction contract

THESIS: Pavcraft's catalogue is the notice board nobody authorised: paper notices taped to a flat acid-lime board, each tool one notice with tear-off tabs, and tearing a tab takes you to it. The page refuses the SaaS grid of identical tool cards and the dark tech hero.

OWN-WORLD: Flat acid-lime board (#D9FF41), no texture, with oversized ghost words ("Рутина", "Маржа", "Ціни") as barely visible background. White office paper (#FFFFFF) and yellowed paper (#F1EEE4) notices with translucent crimped masking tape, dashed tear lines (#9A978E), ink #141414, ballpoint blue (#2B35E8) as the only action colour, marker pink-red (#CF1452) for shouting scribbles. Two handwritten faces: Playpen Sans for paper, Shantell Sans (bounce and informality 100, uppercase) for marker scribbles on the board. Rude Ukrainian scribbles each covered by a peelable censor sticker (round yellow "18+", tape "цензура", price tag "−100%", blue "***"). Notices rotate up to about 2.4 degrees; shadows are drop-shadow filters that follow the paper's own shape (no backing panels).

STORY: The visitor sees an acid-lime board of notices, recognises the form instantly, reads that each notice is a tool that removes one manual chore, finds the one for their field, tears a tab or taps the notice to open it; if nothing fits, the "Що вам заважає?" notice's tabs open an email. They can peel any paper by its tape and stick it elsewhere.

FIRST VIEWPORT: On the lime board, a taped "PAVCRAFT / інструменти для малого бізнесу" tag top left and a taped "Написати" slip top right; the handwritten headline "Оголошення для тих, хто все робить сам" with a note taped under it; marker scribbles around, including "ОГОЛОШЕННЯ НЕ КЛЕЇТИ" under a sticker; then the Pricing Kit notice (heading "Pricing Kit / для продавців Prom", "Відкрити Pricing Kit →" link, tear-off tabs "Pricing Kit", one already torn to a ragged stub) and the "Що вам заважає?" notice beside it on desktop; on phones the Pricing Kit notice with its link is in view.

FORM: Notice board with tear-off notices (paper on a board, the tear as the control), reworked on 2026-11-09 from the stairwell fusion into an acid-lime variant. seed:29c358412a09147b. Raises kept: future tools present as yellowed older notices with "за попитом" tabs ("скоро" dropped: it implied a promise against the demand-check rule); one fixed address per tool (its tab label).

SIGNATURE: Pull to tear and pick up anything. Tabs: on mouse press and pull to tear the tab into a ghost that follows the pointer, release and it falls away and opens the tool; on touch hold still about 260ms to arm, then pull (a plain swipe keeps scrolling); a click also tears and navigates; the cut line never moves. Everything else is draggable: papers by tape or body, marker scribbles by their text (touch needs a 320ms hold), with grab and grabbing cursors; dropped things stick at a new slight angle, positions persist per viewer (desktop and mobile separately), arrow keys move a focused tape, "Повісити все як було" resets. Censor stickers peel off on tap. Motion is skipped under reduced motion; links work with no script.
